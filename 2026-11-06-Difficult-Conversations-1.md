## Synopsis
The authors (Douglass Stone, Bruce Patton,and Sheila Heen) break down all disagreements based upon 3 axis. What Happened (a difference of perspective), Feelings, and Identity (what it means to be me to be in the wrong). The author uses a really good example of a scathing critique of a last minute project, which upon rereading I saw from the other side I hadnt previously seen. The authors then say that instead of having a battle of messages upon these three axis there needs to be a common ground to find to properly resolve disagreements.

Getting not only the story from each side but having each tech acknowledging the others emotions might help during deadlocks. The identity, I feel is a more complex conversation than what any tech will care to have outright. The problem also is that I cant sharpen any of my tools (practice) that this book is trying to give me.
