## Synopsis
The author says that there are three blindspots that contribute to inbibiting to self evaluation. They are knowledge blindspots where we overestimate our ability in areas of poor expertise by assuming we are around average. Emotinoal bilndspots where the emotions that we are experiencing due to x, y, z reasons cloud our judgement. The last is Behavioral blindspots where the actions we take down to body language are interpreted diffently from other bodies that our own, leading to an blindspot in assuming how we are understood. Lastly the author says that these blindspots lead to a list of assumptions that we have without realizing it that need to be identified. Another good idea listed was pre-mortem that supposes when making a plan do not plan for the best but the worst and use the assumed failings to identify weak areas of the plan.
