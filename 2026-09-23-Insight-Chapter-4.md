## Synopsis
The author supposes that there is a universal cult of self that has been adopted into the modern zeitgeist. In this cult of self we have gone from valuing effort to valuing self-esteem. This has led us to view ourselves through rose tinted glasses and overestimate our abilites in areas we are particularly weak at. The author supposes that this has led us to focus on how others see us rather than our effect we have in the workplace. The author suggests the three ways to fight off this modern view is to become informers about the world around us rather than informers of ourselves (meformers), Focus on humility to temper our sucesses. And lastly to focus on self acceptance rather than self-esteem.
