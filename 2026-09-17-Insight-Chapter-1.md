## Synopsis
The author breaks self reflection down to two related but independent fields internal and external. Although these might seem closely related they do not transfer over and very often being good at one of them can come at a cost of the other. The author claims that when we do not have a strong internal self awareness we will take actions that do not align with our actual desires. Also when we do not have a strong external sense of self awareness reality is different from how we perceive it ie delusion.
