## Synopsis
The author supposes that there are seven axioms that can be used to judge what our actual self-awareness is both external and internal. The list is as follows:
Internal:
- Values
- Passions
- Aspirations
- Fit
External:
- Patterns
- Others Reactions
- Impact

The author supposes that our understanding is to be judged off of this list and that just simple goal making can accidentally ignore our internal values, passions, etc... and that just using introspection can ignore the external attributes. We need to analyze how others see our external attributes (patterns, reactions, and impact) to truly understand others understanding of ourselves.
